# Privacy Policy for VoxFab AI Keyboard

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled in the **VoxFab AI Keyboard** mobile application (hereafter referred to as the "App"), an Android keyboard with AI voice dictation. The App is built around a simple principle: **it runs on your own OpenAI API key**. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, and the developer operates no server that receives your typing, your voice or your texts.

---

### 1. What stays on your device
The following never leave your phone and are stored only locally:
* Your OpenAI API key (stored on-device only; never sent anywhere except to OpenAI as authentication for your own requests).
* Your personal dictionary and learned words.
* Your clipboard history (items that look like passwords or keys are never stored).
* All settings, themes and preferences.
* The text you type with the keyboard. Ordinary typing is never transmitted anywhere.

### 2. Voice dictation and AI text features (OpenAI)
When **you** choose to dictate or use the AI text features, the App connects directly from your device to **OpenAI** using **your own API key**:
* **Dictation:** the audio you record is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription. If Smart Formatting is enabled, the transcribed text is also sent to OpenAI (GPT) to add punctuation and formatting.
* **Fix & Continue:** the text you select is sent to OpenAI (GPT) to correct or continue it.
* This data goes from your device straight to OpenAI under your own account and is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy. The developer does not receive, store or have access to your audio or texts. The App does not keep your audio after transcription.

### 3. GIF search
GIF search is keyless. When you search for a GIF, your search words are sent to third-party GIF providers (such as Bing, Tenor or Giphy) to fetch results. Do not search for anything private.

### 4. Update checks
The App periodically checks GitHub for a newer version of itself. This request contains no personal data, no device identifiers and nothing about how you use the keyboard.

### 5. What the App does NOT do
* No accounts or sign-up.
* No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs.
* No data collection by the developer — the developer has no backend and receives nothing from the App.
* Your data is not sold or shared with data brokers. Ever.

### 6. Permissions
* **Microphone** — only while you are dictating.
* **Internet** — for the OpenAI and GIF requests described above, and update checks.
* **Vibrate** — keypress haptics.

### 7. Children's Privacy
The App collects no personal information and is not directed at children under 13.

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be applied directly to this document.
